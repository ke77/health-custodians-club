# 00. Project brief

## What the site is for

Health Custodians Club (HCC) brings together tertiary students from health-related fields to close gaps in healthy-lifestyle awareness and healthcare access through training, research and sensitization programmes.
Members build skills in communication, leadership, languages, research and disease studies, then use them to educate communities in schools, marketplaces, rural communities and other underserved settings.

The site has three jobs:
1. Make HCC understandable and credible.
2. Let eligible students apply.
3. Give advisers, partners, sponsors and health professionals a clear way to get in touch.

## Audiences (in priority order, agreed 15 Oct 2026)

1. **Prospective members**: tertiary students in the health sciences. They should find it easy to understand HCC and apply.
2. **Potential advisers, partners, sponsors and health professionals**: they need to see HCC is credible and have a clear way to get in touch.
3. **Club executives**: they review applications and keep the site up to date after launch.

## How the site presents HCC

- It reads as an established, credible organisation. Do not describe it as student-founded or tied to a single campus.
- No membership fees or dues shown anywhere.
- No invented content: no past events, partners, numbers or testimonials unless the club supplies and approves them.
- Nationwide ambition: representatives in all 16 regions, with sensitization and clean-up outreaches aimed at every two months. Say "aims to" or "planned" where something is not yet running.
- Tone: clear, warm, direct, plain language. The site itself should follow the club's own principle of making health information simple.

## Scope and timeline

- **Phase 1 (MVP)**: essential pages, application form with automatic emails, lean admin dashboard (secure sign-in, application review, deadline and open/closed switch). Target: live before school resumes on 26 October 2026.
- **Phase 2**: remaining dashboard features, added and tested step by step: blog editor, leadership and Board of Advisors editing, partners editing, FAQ editing, site settings, status-change emails, enquiries inbox.
- Design and build the Home page first. The club approves it before anything else is built.
- After Phase 1 there is handoff documentation (see `docs/06-build-plan.md`). The developer is not planning ongoing maintenance, so build for handoff.

## Primary goals of the build

1. A fast, accessible, phone-first site (most visitors are on phones over mobile data in Ghana).
2. A dependable application pipeline that the club can run without a developer.
3. Content that is editable without code changes wherever practical (stored in the database from day one, edited through the dashboard in Phase 2).
4. A design that does not look like a generic AI template.
