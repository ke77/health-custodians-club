## About

Day-in day-out, our world is transforming, health problems keep increasing, healthcare systems keep advancing, yet still there remain some gaps. The basic health challenges are not addressed until they escalate into advanced problems. The 13-year-old boy or girl is unaware of the consequences of unhealthy diet, dehydration, and excessive consumption of sugary drinks on his/her kidney health. The lady or gentleman who closes from work at 7:00 PM and eats heavy meals at 8:00PM has no idea about how that habit can lead to type 2 diabetes. The market women who sell in the open space at the marketplace have no idea about the cause of the constant coughing and sneezing. As a matter of fact, they are unaware of the effects of the prolonged exposure of these environmental toxins and particulate matter on their respiratory health. Because of language barriers, some sick people have chosen self-medication over hospital treatment. Even in the consulting room, patients find it difficult to communicate symptoms to the physician, leading to misdiagnoses. People may know that something is unhealthy without understanding how much, how often, and how often it increases disease risk.

Health Custodians Club (HCC) is established to bring together students from different fields of health to address these gaps in healthy lifestyle and healthcare through training, research, and sensitization programs. Members will be equipped with essential skills in communication, leadership, languages, research, and disease studies, enabling them to effectively educate, engage, and empower communities. Through these skills, members will be prepared to contribute to addressing gaps in healthy lifestyle practices and healthcare access by promoting health awareness, disease prevention, early detection, and informed health-seeking behaviors.

## Vision

To be a leading hub for developing the next generation of health professionals by equipping health students with the practical, professional, leadership, communication, research, innovation, and interpersonal skills that extend beyond the traditional university curriculum, preparing them to excel in their careers, serve communities effectively, and contribute to transforming health outcomes globally.

## Mission

1. Empowering members to discover, shape, and pursue their visions and aspirations, equipping them with the knowledge, skills, networks, and opportunities needed to achieve their goals in the health industry.
2. Contributing to the reduction of the burden of health problems in Africa through health education, sensitization programs, community outreach, and advocacy in schools, marketplaces, rural communities, and other underserved settings.
3. Developing the next generation of disciplined, purpose-driven, innovative, and results-oriented leaders in the health industry through leadership development, mentorship, professional development, and practical community engagement.
4. Making health information simple, understandable, and accessible to everyone, regardless of their educational or professional background, so that individuals and communities can make informed decisions about their health.
5. Promoting a culture of research, innovation, and evidence-based health advocacy by encouraging members to identify health challenges, develop solutions, and use reliable evidence to address community health needs.
6. Building a network of health students, health professionals, researchers, and other stakeholders who collaborate to promote health, share knowledge, mentor young professionals, and create meaningful impact in communities.

## Organizational structure

### Board of Advisors

Roles:

- Provide strategic advice to club executives
- Assist with networking and partnerships
- Mentor club executives
- Support sponsorship efforts
- Help ensure continuity and professionalism

### Executive Committee

- President
- Vice President
- General Secretary
- Financial Secretary
- Organizing Secretary
- Public Relations Officer

Executive roles will be based on appointments depending on the commitment level of individuals who will show interest.

## Membership

- Strictly for tertiary students with background in health sciences
- Individuals will apply and go through an application process before membership can be secured.
- There shall be presiding heads for members in all the 16 regions in the country.
- Graduation is after every 2 years, with an official certificate for every member.
- Alumni members are always welcome to support club activities.
